## C. Ordering rationale (read once)

Public shell first because it has zero data dependencies and gets a real site live fast. Identity schema (S2) before auth (S3) because Apply writes `profiles` + `applications` on day one. The approval gate (S4) immediately after auth because **no gated content may ship before the gate is proven** — that's why content schema (S5) and private pages (S6) come after. Private pages (S6) complete the MVP product, so **launch (S7) comes next** — the owner ships on the Vercel domain immediately after S6. **Stage 3 is post-launch.** Workspace Visual Polish (S8) and Live Programming (S9) are merged. **Replanned 2026-06-26 (D-S10-b):** make the partner platform crystal-clear and premium (S10) → give the client full content control (S11) → wire the email automations as ready-to-switch-on placeholders (S12).
